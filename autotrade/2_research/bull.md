## 4) Let’s address the bear case directly

### Bear argument #1: “SPY is overbought and due for a reversal.”
My response: **overbought can stay overbought in a powerful trend.**

The report suggests RSI is likely elevated, and price may be pressing the upper Bollinger Band. That usually signals a strong trend — not necessarily a sell signal. In fact, in trending markets:
- RSI can stay elevated for extended periods,
- price can ride the upper band,
- and mean reversion often never becomes severe enough to justify a short unless support breaks.

So yes, the index may be stretched. But stretch alone is not a bearish thesis. It’s only bearish if the tape confirms exhaustion, and so far it hasn’t.

### Bear argument #2: “Volume is fading, so the rally lacks conviction.”

If anything, moderate volume during a high-level consolidation can be constructive because it suggests there is no panic distribution. Selling pressure is not overwhelming the market.
